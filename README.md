# 📡 Jan Cable TV & Internet App

**Jan Cable TV & Internet App** is a lightweight Android application developed by **Adeeb Technology Lab** that allows users to easily view and manage their cable television and internet subscription details.  
This app is useful for subscribers of local cable/internet services to track their **data usage, remaining quota, package expiry, and plan details** directly on their mobile device. 

---

## 📍 About

The **Jan Cable TV & Internet App** is designed to present key information about your cable and internet subscription in one place. Users can:

- 📊 Check remaining **data/internet quota**
- 📅 See **package expiration dates**
- ⚡ View **connection speed** and plan details
- 📱 Navigate a simple and intuitive interface

It’s ideal for casual internet users as well as power users who want quick access to their plan status without logging into a web portal.
